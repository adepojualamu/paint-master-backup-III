Our story

From frustration to standard

In 2024, our founder paid three different quotes for the same living room — each a different price, each a different person. Two painters didn't show up. The third did, but the "premium paint" was a diluted mix, and the finish lasted four months before it started chalking.

Paint Masters was founded on the belief that Ghana deserves the same professional service standard as any city in the world. That means trained artisans, fair fixed pricing, a warranty, and a real company behind the work.

FAQ

Frequently asked

How does the warranty work?

If you notice peeling, blistering, or chalking within 12 months of the QA sign-off, message us with your booking reference and a photo. A Paint Master will be scheduled within 14 business days to redo the affected area — at no charge.

What's included in a standard quote?

Labour, paint (standard grade by default — premium and luxury are options), prep materials (tape, masking, primer, putty), furniture protection, and clean-up. VAT is included. Travel is included for Accra Metro; outside cities get a location-based surcharge.

How does escrow payment work?

At booking you pay a 40% deposit to our licensed escrow partner (Paystack). The remaining 60% is authorised when you book, but only released to us after your QA inspection passes. If anything's wrong, we fix it before we're paid.

Do you serve outside Accra?

Yes — Accra Metro, Tema, Kasoa, Kumasi, Takoradi, and Cape Coast. Travel surcharges apply outside Accra Metro and are shown on the quote before you book.

Can I choose my own paint brand?

Absolutely. We default to premium Dulux or Leyland, but you can request any brand (Azar, Coral, Berger, or imported) in the quote form. Price adjusts accordingly.

What if I need to cancel?

Free cancellation up to 48 hours before the scheduled start. Inside 48 hours, a 15% restocking fee applies (materials are usually already ordered).
