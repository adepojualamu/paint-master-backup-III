Pricing

The rate card behind every quote. Finance sub-role required to edit.
These values are the single source of truth for what a job costs. The quote calculator a customer sees and the price they're charged both read from here — there is no separate hidden formula. A quote total is built as: labour (day-rate × days + area × the service's per-m² rate) + materials (area × the materials rate) + platform fee (% of labour), then VAT is added on top. Changes apply to new quotes the moment you save.
