Frequently asked

How do I assign a paint master to a new job?

From the Job Board, drag the job card from the New Quote column into Scheduled, then click the card to choose a paint master. The system filters masters by region and current load. Once assigned, the job moves to Dispatched and the master gets an SMS.

What does the "Reset demo data" button do?

It clears the local demo state (jobs, customers, inventory) saved in this browser and restores the seeded sample data. It does not affect anything on the server.

How are platform fees and refunds calculated?

Default platform fee is 10% of the booking total. Cancellations more than 48 hours before the job get a full refund; later cancellations get a partial refund (80% of total). Both values are configurable in Settings → Pricing.

Why do I see "PM" instead of the official logo?

That's the fallback shown when the logo file is missing. Drop the official logo at assets/img/paint-masters-logo.png and refresh — it will appear everywhere automatically (nav, footer, sidebar, login).

How do I add another admin user?

For now, sign-up flow is customer/painter only. To create another admin, run the create-admin script on the server: node backend/scripts/create-admin.js --phone 0244000001 --name "Akosua B."

Inventory shows "low" — what's the threshold?

Each item has a reorder quantity. When stock falls at or below that, the badge appears in the sidebar. Edit reorder thresholds inline on the Inventory page.

How do I reset my password?

From Settings → Security, enter your current password and a new one. If you've forgotten the current password, use the Forgot password link on the sign-in page (rolling out next release — meanwhile, contact support below).
