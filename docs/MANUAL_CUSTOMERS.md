# Customer Training Manual

Welcome to Paint Masters — the easiest way to book a certified painter in Ghana. This guide walks you through everything you'll do on the platform: getting a quote, booking your first job, tracking progress while it's running, and claiming your warranty if you ever need it.

The whole flow takes about ten minutes the first time, two minutes after that. No app to install, no calls to make. Everything happens through the website on your phone or laptop.

---

## 1. Getting an instant quote

Open the homepage. The big yellow **Get Instant Quote →** button takes you to the quote builder.

You'll be asked four things:

- **The service.** Pick from Interior Wall Repaint, Exterior Refresh, Decorative Faux Finish, Roof Coating, or one of our specialty options. If you're unsure, pick the closest match — your assigned Paint Master will adjust on site.
- **The area in square metres.** Don't have an exact number? Use the rule of thumb the page shows: a typical bedroom is about 35 m², a small living room is 50 m², a hallway is 12 m².
- **Whether materials are included.** "Yes" means we bring the paint and primer. "No" means you've already bought them and we just supply labour.
- **Your region.** Drives the travel surcharge (zero in Accra, more in other cities).

The total updates live as you change inputs. The breakdown shows labour, materials, travel, and the 12.5% VAT line separately, so you can see exactly where every cedi goes.

When you're happy with the number, click **Book this →** and we'll carry the quote forward into the booking flow.

## 2. Booking the job

The booking page asks for:

- **Your address.** As specific as possible — "12 Boundary Road, Cantonments" beats just "East Legon".
- **The date and time window.** Morning, Afternoon, or All-day. We schedule by half-day blocks so the Master arrives within an hour of the window opening.
- **A delivery contact phone.** This is the number we'll call if the Master gets held up in traffic.
- **Any access notes.** Side gate code, dog warning, parking instructions, the works. The Master sees these on their app before they arrive.

When you submit the booking, you'll see a **booking reference** like `PM-482015`. Keep this — it's how you'll find the project tracker later, and it's the only thing you'll need for a warranty claim.

You'll get an SMS confirming the booking and an email with the receipt. (Email setup is configured per deployment — if you don't see one, your dispatcher hasn't finished their email setup yet.)

## 3. Paying for the job

Paint Masters is **strictly cashless**. We accept:

- **Mobile Money** (MTN MoMo, Telecel, AirtelTigo) — the most common choice, takes about 30 seconds.
- **Bank card** through the Hubtel checkout — slower (the bank's 3-D Secure step adds a minute) but works for international cards.

You'll see a payment-method picker on the booking confirmation page. Pick MoMo and a USSD prompt arrives on your phone within 5 seconds; enter your MoMo PIN to approve.

**Your money goes into escrow, not directly to the Master.** It only releases to them after our Quality Assurance team has inspected the finished job AND a 7-day holdback period has passed. If there's a defect, the holdback gives us time to redo the work before any money has moved. This is how the cashless guarantee actually protects you.

If a Master ever asks you for cash on the side, **don't pay them**. Open the project tracker and tap the red **⚠ Report a cash request** button — dispatch will pull them off your job and reassign within the hour at no extra cost.

## 4. Tracking the job in progress

After booking, sign in at **paintmasters.gh/login.html** and click the **Track Project** link in the top nav. You'll land on the live tracker for your most recent booking.

The tracker shows you, at a glance:

- **Where the job is right now** — Scheduled, Master Dispatched, On site, Work in progress, QA Check, or Completed.
- **A timeline** with timestamps for every stage transition.
- **Your assigned Paint Master** — their name, photo, star rating, and specialties.
- **Progress photos** — your Master uploads them through the day. You can also upload your own (see below).
- **Materials and hours logged** — what's been used, who's been on site.

### The chat panel

Right side of the tracker. Talk to your dispatcher, or to the Paint Master directly. There are three send modes:

- **In-platform** (default) — message appears in the chat thread, dispatcher and Master both see it.
- **SMS** — opens your phone's text-message app pre-filled. The dispatcher gets it as a regular SMS.
- **WhatsApp** — same idea via WhatsApp Web or the app.

Pick whichever's easiest. Use SMS or WhatsApp if you want a fast reply on the road.

### Action buttons

Below the chat:

- **📸 Request a specific photo** — describe the spot ("the corner above the door, I want to check the seam"). Both the Master and dispatch see the request and reply.
- **📝 Change scope (needs approval)** — tell us about a change ("add the second-floor hallway"). Both Master and admin must approve before it takes effect; we'll send a revised quote.
- **⭐ Leave a review (after QA)** — open after the job is QA-passed. Pick 1–5 stars, write a comment, drag-and-drop before/after photos from your tracker into the review.
- **⚠ Report a cash request** — only use if a Master asks you for cash. Pulls them off the job, calls you back within 30 minutes.
- **⚠ Report a concern** — anything else not right. Quality, safety, behaviour, or property damage. A senior dispatcher follows up within 60 minutes if you tick "call me back".

### Uploading your own progress photos

The progress-photo grid on the tracker has an **+ Add photos** tile. Drop image files on it (JPEG, PNG, WebP, GIF — up to 8 MB each).

Every photo you upload goes into a **moderation queue first** — the admin reviews it before it shows on your dashboard, so the tracker stays clean and on-topic. You'll see a small **⏳ Pending review** badge in the corner of any photo waiting for review. Approved photos lose the badge and become visible to your Master too.

If a photo is rejected (rare — happens for non-relevant images, blurry shots, or accidental personal photos), you'll see a **✕ Rejected** badge with the reason.

## 5. After the job — your warranty

Every Paint Masters job comes with a **12-month finish warranty**. It covers premature peeling, blistering, flaking, cracking, colour drift, and trim-detail issues attributable to application.

When the job moves to the **Completed** stage, you'll see two things:

- The tracker's "Your warranty" card unlocks a **📄 Download certificate** button. Click it; a styled certificate opens in a new tab with your name, the booking ref, the painter, the address, and the cover window. Hit **⬇ Download PDF** in the toolbar (or use the browser's File → Print → Save as PDF). Keep the file — we'll need the certificate number if you make a claim.
- The same certificate appears on your account dashboard under **Warranty certificates** at any time. Each row has a **📄 View / download** link.

### Making a warranty claim

If something fails within 12 months:

1. Open your project tracker (or `my.html`).
2. Tap **⚠ Report a concern** and pick category "Quality of work".
3. Describe what failed and attach a photo if you can.
4. Email `warranty@paintmasters.gh` with the certificate number if you'd rather use email.

A senior dispatcher confirms the claim, schedules a senior Paint Master to reinspect, and we redo the affected work at no cost. The warranty covers the original scope only — work added after the original job is covered for 12 months from that work's completion date.

## 6. Your account dashboard

Sign in and click your name in the top right → **My account**. The dashboard shows:

- **Active jobs** — anything currently in progress.
- **Completed** — your full job history, with rebook-this links.
- **Warranty cover** — every active certificate with months remaining.
- **Avg satisfaction** — the average star rating across your reviews.

You can update your profile (name, phone, email) from the **Edit** link next to your details. Changes apply immediately.

## 7. The brand pages

The homepage's "brands we paint with" section links each partner brand to its own landing page. The brand page shows:

- The brand's story and what we use it for.
- A curated colour palette — tap any swatch to copy the hex code or pull the colour into a quote.
- The paints from that brand currently in stock.

Caparol gets extended treatment — they're our anchor partner, so jobs done with Caparol carry an **18-month warranty** (six months longer than standard) plus in-store custom colour matching while you wait.
