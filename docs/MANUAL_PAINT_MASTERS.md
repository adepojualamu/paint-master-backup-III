# Paint Master Training Manual

This is your day-to-day guide to the Paint Masters platform. Everything you need to do as a Master happens through the website at **paintmasters.gh** — sign in once, and every job, calendar slot, customer message, and earnings figure lives in the same place.

The platform isn't an app — it's a website that works the same on your phone, tablet, or laptop. If you can open WhatsApp Web, you can use this.

---

## 1. Your first sign-in

After admin approves your application:

1. You'll get an **SMS with your phone number, a temporary password, and a sign-in link**. The temp password is short on purpose (something like `PM-AKO-2847`) so it's easy to type once.
2. Open the link, enter your phone number and the temp password.
3. The platform sends you straight to a **change-password** page — pick a new password (six characters or more), type it twice, click **Save and continue**.
4. You land on your **Paint Master dashboard** at `/painter.html`. This is your home page from now on.

The temp password is single-use — once you've changed it, the SMS one stops working.

If you ever forget your new password, contact dispatch via SMS or WhatsApp. They'll issue a new temp and you go through the same flow.

## 2. The Paint Master dashboard

Bookmark the dashboard. Five sections:

### Today's assignment

Big card at the top. Shows the customer, address, service, and time window for any job scheduled today.

Two buttons depending on the stage:
- **📍 Check in on site** — tap when you arrive at the customer's location. Stamps the time, notifies dispatch, and unlocks the next stage.
- **✓ Mark QA-ready** — tap when you've finished. Triggers our QA team to schedule an inspection. Don't tap this until you've cleaned up; QA failure means a return visit.

If there's no job today, the card switches to a friendly empty state with the next scheduled job called out.

### Stat row

Four numbers at a glance:
- **Today** — how many jobs scheduled for today.
- **This week** — jobs through Sunday.
- **On-time %** — your last 90-day average. Aim for 95%+.
- **Customer rating** — your average star rating. Anything under 4.5 triggers a coaching conversation with dispatch.

### 28-day calendar

Full month grid, Mon-start, today outlined in navy. Booked days are gold; busy/leave days are red; free days are grey. Tap any booked tile to open a popover with the job's address, customer, window, duration, and quoted total — plus stage-aware quick actions (check in, mark QA-ready, open the full job).

### Upcoming jobs

The next 5 jobs on your books, in date order. Click any row to open the same popover.

### Earnings — this month

Live numbers driven by your completed jobs. The platform takes a 10% fee, holds 5% of the net as a warranty reserve, and pays out the remaining 95% of net. So if you complete GHS 1,000 of jobs in a month:

- Gross: GHS 1,000
- Net to you (90%): GHS 900
- Warranty hold (5% of net): GHS 45
- Released to you now: GHS 855

The warranty hold is **your money** — it's released back to you 90 days after the job, with no claim. The dashboard shows you the running total of held warranty across all your jobs.

Phase 2 (coming soon) automates the actual payout via Hubtel. Today the admin processes payouts manually — see "Getting paid" below.

## 3. The notifications panel

Right side of the dashboard. Streams real-time events that affect you:

- **New job assigned · PM-XXXXXX** — admin assigned a job to you. Card shows the customer, service, and start/end dates. Click "View" to open the popover.
- **Job dispatched · PM-XXXXXX** — admin gave the green light to start. Show up at the start window.
- **Customer message** — when a customer messages you on a job's tracker.
- **Customer review** — after they post one (good or bad).
- **Concerns / cash requests** — if a customer reports a concern about your work or claims you asked for cash. These are urgent — read them immediately.

A red dot on the bell shows unread count. Tap "Mark all read" to clear it.

## 4. Doing a job — the full flow

Step-by-step for a typical Interior Wall Repaint:

### Day before

1. **Check the dashboard** in the evening. Today's-assignment card shows tomorrow's job if you have one.
2. **Read the job notes** in the popover. Customer's access notes (gate code, dog, parking) live there.
3. **Stock up.** Materials and brushes you'll need are in the customer's quote — admin adjusts stock for you.

### Morning of

1. **Open the dashboard.** Today's assignment moves to the top.
2. **Tap 📍 Check in on site** as you arrive. Stamps the time, dispatch sees you've arrived, customer sees your status flip to "On site" on their tracker.
3. **Take a "BEFORE" photo** with your phone. Upload it via the customer's tracker (or hand it to admin to upload from their console). The customer also gets a **+ Add photos** tile so they can ask you to capture specific spots.
4. **Do the work.** Take 1–2 progress photos through the day so the customer sees the job moving.

### Mid-job

- **Customer asks for a specific photo?** You'll see a `[Photo request]` message in the chat. Snap it on your next break and reply.
- **Customer wants to add scope?** They'll send a `[Scope change]` message with the request. **Don't agree to anything off the books.** Reply with your estimate and tell them admin will confirm in writing — admin then sends them a revised quote. If they don't approve in writing, don't do the extra work.
- **Customer offers cash?** Politely decline. Tell them payment is through escrow and the job is already paid. If they push, mention you can be reported and removed from the platform — that usually ends it. If they keep pushing, finish the original scope and tell admin afterwards.

### End of job

1. **Clean up properly.** Drop cloths folded, masking tape removed, no paint on furniture or flooring. Take an "AFTER" photo.
2. **Tap ✓ Mark QA-ready** on the dashboard's today card. Status flips to "QA Check".
3. **Wait for QA inspection.** Usually within 24 hours. If they pass, the customer gets a notification and the 7-day holdback timer starts.
4. **The customer will be invited to leave a review.** Don't pressure them — but do hand them a card with the platform name as you leave so they remember to do it.

## 5. The customer chat

When a customer is talking to you on their tracker, you see the messages tagged **`Kofi (Master)`** in their chat (replacing your name). Reply through the same chat — it lands in their tracker tagged the same way.

The chat supports three send modes from the customer side. They might message you via SMS or WhatsApp instead of in-platform — those land in your phone's regular SMS or WhatsApp app and you reply from there. The platform shows a system receipt in the customer's tracker confirming the message went via that channel.

## 6. Calendar and availability

Your calendar updates whenever admin assigns or dispatches a job to you. You don't manually create events.

To **mark yourself unavailable** (vacation, sick, training):

1. Tell dispatch via SMS or in-platform chat.
2. Admin uses the painter modal to flip your status to "On leave" — your calendar shows red for those days and you stop appearing in the assign-job picker.

When you're back, message admin to flip you to "Available". The calendar clears.

## 7. Getting paid

Two flows — today's manual one and the future automated one.

### Today (Phase 1, manual)

End of each calendar week, admin opens the **Painter Payouts** page in their console. They see:

- Your name, region, specialties.
- How many jobs you completed in the period.
- Gross earned, net (90%), warranty hold (5%), and your payable amount.
- An override field if there's a special situation (advance, deduction, settlement).
- A **Mark paid** button.

When admin marks you paid, they send the money via Hubtel directly from their MoMo wallet to yours. You'll get a confirmation SMS from Hubtel.

If your numbers don't match what you expected: pull up the dashboard's earnings card on the same day, screenshot it, and bring it to dispatch. They'll cross-check against the admin payout page.

### Future (Phase 2, automated)

When the Hubtel Send Money integration ships, the admin's "Mark paid" button becomes "Send via Hubtel" — they click once and the disbursement happens server-side, with no manual MoMo step. Same numbers, faster turnaround.

PAYE and SSNIT will be deducted at source on monthly payroll once Phase 2 is live. You'll get a payslip per month showing gross, deductions, and net.

## 8. Your reviews and rating

Every review the customer leaves shows up:

- Live in your **Notifications** panel as a card.
- In your average rating on the dashboard's stat row.
- On the public **artisans page** (`/artisans.html`) where future customers pick a Master.

Aim for 4.8+ across the last 30 days. Anything under 4.5 means dispatch will pull you in for a coaching session.

If a review is unfair (customer was unreasonable, or the rating is at odds with what they wrote), reply through the in-platform chat — admin can mark it under review and respond on your behalf.

## 9. Common things you'll need to do

- **See my calendar** → Bookmark `/painter.html` or click "My calendar & jobs" in the nav dropdown.
- **Reply to a customer** → Open the job's tracker (View button on the notification card), then chat in the right panel.
- **Check what materials I logged** → Open the job's popover; the materials line shows what was used.
- **See my earnings this month** → Dashboard, right column.
- **Report a customer issue** → Use the in-platform chat with `[Concern]` in the message — admin sees it as high-priority.
- **Request a day off** → SMS dispatch with the date(s); they'll flip your status.
- **Update your phone or email** → Tell admin; for security reasons painters can't edit their own contact info today.

## 10. Getting help

In order of speed:

1. **In-platform chat on a job's tracker** — fastest if the issue is about that job.
2. **WhatsApp dispatch** — `+233 54 000 0000`, Mon–Sat 7am–8pm.
3. **SMS dispatch** — same number, slower in busy hours.
4. **Email support** — `support@paintmasters.gh`, 4-hour response window.

If something's urgent (you're locked out, customer is hostile, safety issue), call the dispatch line directly. Don't wait for chat.
